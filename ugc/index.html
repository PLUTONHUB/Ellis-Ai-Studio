About

Content that feels lived, not staged.

Building mind, body & freedom.

Jacob Ellis creates cinematic, short-form content for brands across fitness, wellness, tech, travel and lifestyle — shot the way he actually lives: early trail runs, outdoor training, waterfront cities, and the quiet in-between moments.

Every piece blends storytelling, authentic experience and clean product demonstration — content that reads as a real moment first, and an ad second. Jake can deliver both organic-feeling creator content and performance-oriented advertising creative, built to be edited, remixed and run as paid social.

He's also the founder of Ellis AI Studio, which shapes how he approaches tech and productivity content — he understands the product, the market, and the pitch, not just the camera.

Creative Style

Content that feels lived, not staged.

Every piece follows the same underlying system, whether it's a 9-second hook or a full day-in-the-life edit.

01

Problem / emotion

What the viewer is actually feeling before they know they need the product.

02

Real-life environment

City street, trail, gym, marina, hotel room — or a controlled setup when a brand needs one. The environment serves the story.

03

Product integration

Used the way it's actually used, in the moment it actually gets used.

04

Experience

What changed — physically, mentally, or in the moment — shown, not narrated.

05

Outcome / CTA

A clear, earned close — built to convert without breaking the tone of everything before it.

"The best UGC doesn't look like an ad wearing a lifestyle costume. It looks like a lifestyle that happens to include the product."
