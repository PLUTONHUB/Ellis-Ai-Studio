The bag is in the price

Cabin bag allowance is chosen at search, not at checkout. The number you compare is the number you pay.

Extras are opt-in, once

Seats, priority and insurance are offered on a single screen with a visible “no thanks”. They are never pre-ticked.

No countdown timers

Manufactured urgency costs more in abandoned baskets than it wins in conversions. The fare holds while you decide.
