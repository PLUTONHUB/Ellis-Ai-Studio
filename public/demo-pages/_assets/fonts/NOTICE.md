# Fonts used by the concept redesigns

These variable `.woff2` files are the `latin` subsets published by
[Fontsource](https://fontsource.org/). They are vendored here — rather than
loaded from a CDN — so every file under `demos/` renders identically offline
and screenshots are byte-for-byte reproducible.

Every family below is licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**, which
