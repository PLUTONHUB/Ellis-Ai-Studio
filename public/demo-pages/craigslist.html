Dealing safely

Deal locally, face to face.

Nearly every scam can be avoided by meeting the other person. That advice has been on craigslist for two decades — it should be impossible to miss, not buried in a help article.

Meet in a public placeMany police departments offer a monitored exchange zone. Bring someone with you.
Never wire moneyNo legitimate buyer or seller asks for a wire transfer, gift cards or a payment app to a stranger.
Inspect before you paySee the item, or the apartment, in person. Do not pay a deposit for something you have not stood in.
